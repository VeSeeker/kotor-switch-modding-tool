# Kotor 1 Switch Modding Guide
#### By VeSeeker

## 1 Introduction
#### You don't have to read this, but you may have questions if you don't

This guide is based on the neocities [KOTOR 1 Full Build for PC](https://kotor.neocities.org/modding/mod_builds/k1/full). I have gotten the majority of the mods in this guide to work on the Switch. You will need a modded Switch, check [here](https://switch.hacks.guide/) for information on that, or an emulator.

Beyond the need for a modded Switch, there are a few things that make modding Kotor on the Switch more difficult. One thing is that the Switch version ships with many `.tpc` textures in it's override folder. Those textures won't be overridden by `.tga` or `.dds` files we put into our override folder, so we have to convert them to `.tpc`.

On the topic of texture files, many of the mods in this build include 4K textures. Although quite the improvement, they end up causing this. Thus, we downscale those textures by 50% in this build. You could probably min-max this by only downscaling half of them or something, but I haven't bothered figuring this out. If you are worried about the quality loss, don't be. The resulting 2K textures are still a massive improvement over the originals. Some modules get extremely close to the VRAM limit, so follow this guide closely.
